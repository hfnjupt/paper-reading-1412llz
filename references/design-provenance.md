- Academic Research Skills：研究—写作—评审流程、证据完整性和用户检查点。https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills
- Research Paper / read-research-paper：多来源论文输入和视觉化阅读产物。https://github.com/aniketkrs/research-paper
- Deep Reader：多遍阅读、页码锚点和忠实重建后再评价。https://github.com/tronghieu/agent-skills/tree/main/skills/deep-reader
- paper-review.skill：证据化批评和外部引用核验。https://github.com/jam-cc/paper-review.skill
